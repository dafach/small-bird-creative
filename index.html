FAQ

Before you email

Who is this for?

Housing authorities and affordable-housing owners who need to fill LIHTC vacancies: clear property sites, bilingual reach, waitlist growth, and ongoing digital ops.

Do you only build property vacancy sites?

Vacancy sites are the core. We also handle ongoing site ops, vacancy campaigns, bilingual phone answering, paid social, collateral, and authority-site copy when you ask for it.

Are the pages bilingual?

Yes. Property pages are built in English and Spanish so more applicants can read them and apply.

What about the phone?

A 24/7 bilingual virtual receptionist through Smith.ai answers in English or Spanish. Phone-first for now. Callers are qualified against the site forms so your team gets usable leads.
