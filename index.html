FAQ

Before you email

Who is this for?

Housing authorities that need to fill LIHTC vacancies: vacancy and waitlist pages, lead routing, bilingual phone intake, ongoing site operations, and property marketing consulting.

What is included in ongoing site operations?

Domains, live property sites, copy turns in two business days or less, and receptionist lines in the operator fee. $650 per property per month.

Where do leads go?

Lead capture routes to Housing Cafe and Adam (housingcafe@hasbarco.org and adam@empowerpartner.co).

Are the pages bilingual?

Yes. Vacancy and waitlist pages are StoryBrand EN/ES with qualify CTAs so more applicants can read them and apply.

What about the phone?

A bilingual virtual receptionist through Smith.ai: click-to-call, English and Spanish, 24/7, on dedicated numbers.

What does property marketing consulting cover?

Org research and outreach, messaging and UX, and print/digital support. On this engagement, phase two does not include events. $3,500 per property-month.
