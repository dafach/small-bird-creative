FAQ

Before you email

Who is this for?

Housing authorities and property teams that need clear vacancy pages, bilingual reach, intake routing, or a phone line that answers in Spanish when needed.

Do you only build property vacancy sites?

Vacancy and lease-up pages are the core. We also handle intake, phone answering, ongoing site ops, property outreach, and authority-site or campaign content when you ask for it.

Are the pages bilingual?

Yes. Property pages are built in English and Spanish so more applicants can read them and apply.

What about the phone?

Dedicated receptionist lines for properties, including Spanish- capable answering, so callers reach a person instead of a dead end.
